How to play

Take turns rolling six dice. After each roll, set aside scoring dice, then either roll the remaining dice or bank your points. If a roll contains no scoring dice, you farkle: the points gathered this turn are lost, and if it was a fresh throw of all six dice (and you are already on the board) you also lose 2,000 from your score. If all six dice score, you get hot dice: roll all six again.

Every three of a kind in a roll must be taken and validated — you cannot leave it on the table.

The first player to reach exactly 10,000 wins on the spot. Banking a total that would pass 10,000 is not allowed — you must keep rolling instead.
