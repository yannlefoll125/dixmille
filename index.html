How to play

Take turns rolling six dice. After each roll, set aside at least one scoring die, then either roll the remaining dice or bank your points. If a roll contains no scoring dice, you farkle and lose everything gathered this turn. If all six dice score, you get hot dice: roll all six again. First to 10,000 triggers the last round; the highest total wins.
